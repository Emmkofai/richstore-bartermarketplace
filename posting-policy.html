Marketplace Trust & Safety

Product Posting & Content Moderation Policy

Effective Date: September 2026  |  Applicable to: All Sellers, Merchants & Listed Items

1. Purpose & Core Principles

RichStore is committed to maintaining a trustworthy, transparent, and respectful e-commerce marketplace. Every item listed on RichStore must adhere to strict ethical standards, accurate product representations, and complete commercial transparency. This policy outlines what is strictly prohibited, the automated and human checks enforced by our system, and the penalties for violations.

2. Strictly Prohibited Content (Immediate System Rejection)

Zero-Tolerance Policy: Any listing falling under the prohibited categories below is automatically rejected by our automated intake filters, suppressed from public catalog indexing, and subject to immediate seller account sanctions.

2.1 Nudity, Pornography & Adult Content

RichStore strictly bans all adult entertainment, pornography, and sexually suggestive or explicit content. Prohibited submissions include:

  • Photographs, illustrations, or graphics depicting human genitalia, buttocks, bare breasts, or sexual acts.
  • Pornographic media, films, adult magazines, explicit literature, or access links to external adult websites.
  • Sex toys, erotic novelties, bondage gear, or adult-themed fetish wear.
  • Apparel modeled with transparent fabric exposing intimate body parts or suggestive pose photography.

2.2 Sexual Exploitation & Non-Consensual Material

RichStore maintains an absolute zero-tolerance protocol regarding sexual exploitation, human trafficking, and harassment:

  • Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM): Any depiction, representation, or text referencing minors in any sexual context will trigger an immediate permanent system ban, IP blacklist, and immediate reporting to law enforcement and international child protection authorities.
  • Non-consensual imagery, upskirt photos, hidden camera captures, or voyeuristic media.
  • Services of a sexual nature, companionship with sexual innuendo, or sexual solicitation.

2.3 Unclear, Blurry, or Obscured Product Images

Buyers have the fundamental right to inspect clear, authentic photos of what they are purchasing. The system rejects:

  • Low Resolution / Blurry Images: Photos below 400×400 pixels or heavily pixelated/compressed files where details, branding, or condition cannot be discerned.
  • Heavily Obstructed Images: Photos covered by heavy watermarks, promotional stamps, contact phone numbers across the image, or visual stickers hiding product flaws.
  • Blank, Irrelevant, or Placeholder Graphics: Submissions utilizing generic "No Image Available", memes, cartoon icons, or screenshots of other website shopping carts.
  • Poor Lighting: Completely dark or overexposed photos that prevent accurate visual inspection of item condition.

2.4 Description Not Matching Product Image (Bait-and-Switch & Deception)

To eliminate fraudulent listings, the system enforces complete semantic and contextual fidelity between product photos and descriptions:

  • Product Discrepancies: Listing an image of a luxury smartphone while writing a description for a phone case, or displaying shoes while the title describes a laptop.
  • Brand Mismatch / Counterfeits: Showing an authentic branded item photo while describing it as a generic replica, or vice-versa.
  • Condition Concealment: Uploading stock promotional photos for an item marked as "Used - Fair" without disclosing physical defects, scratches, or wear shown in the photo.
  • Keyword Stuffing & Clickbait: Padding the description with unrelated brands, tags, or search keywords to artificially game search rankings.

3. Mandatory Transparency & Account Trust Policy

Transparency Guarantee: Buyers are protected by end-to-end verified seller traceability, automated account withholding, and direct 24-hour non-delivery refunds.
🛡️ Trust Policy Note: Account Funds Protection

To guarantee zero risk of fraud for buyers and guaranteed payout for sellers, RichStore operates an escrow account protection mechanism:

  • Funds Securely Withheld: Platform site charges are applied to the amount payable by the buyer at checkout (Seller Listed Price + Platform Site Charge). Site charges apply only when payment from the buyer has been confirmed received. 100% of the funds are held securely in platform account and displayed in real time on both buyer and seller dashboards. No money is paid to the seller upfront.
  • Delivery Confirmation & Payout: Funds remain withheld until the seller delivers the item and the buyer confirms receipt. Upon confirmation, the seller's full listed price is disbursed to their verified mobile money/bank account, and the site fee is routed to the platform's Telecel account.
  • 24-Hour Non-Delivery Refund: If the seller fails to deliver within 24 hours or the buyer cancels an unfulfilled order, the entire payment is refunded back to the buyer's account.
✓ Both buyer and seller see the exact account balance and order status throughout the transaction.
  • Verified Phone Number (OTP Mandatory): Every seller must verify their mobile phone number via One-Time Password (OTP) prior to listing any item. The verified number is permanently attached to the listing and cannot be spoofed.
  • Physical Seller Address: Sellers must disclose a verifiable location/city on the item listing so buyers understand shipping and pickup distances.
  • Transparent Stock & Pricing: Sellers must accurately declare available stock. Selling more quantity than physical stock available is prohibited and automatically blocked by our atomic checkout queue.
  • Direct WhatsApp & Call Traceability: Orders generate instant, direct contact channels between buyers and sellers to ensure verifiable communication.
  • Platform Payout Accountability: All store usage charges, escrow releases, and site fee disbursements are logged in a tamper-proof treasury ledger.

4. How the System Detects & Rejects Violations

RichStore deploys a multi-stage automated moderation pipeline to ensure violating listings never reach the public feed:

1
Real-Time Text Lexicon Screening:

The system scans the Title, Description, and Category against an automated blocklist of adult terminology, sexually explicit keywords, and exploitation triggers. Flagged submissions are rejected instantly at form submission.

2
Automated Image Quality & Dimension Verification:

Uploaded files are inspected using the Sharp image processing pipeline. Files smaller than minimum dimension thresholds, corrupted files, or non-image payloads are refused with an explicit error: "Image rejected: Image too blurry or below 400×400px resolution."

3
Image-Description Heuristic Cross-Validation:

The intake engine verifies that product categories match the textual keywords and image properties. Listings showing high dissonance (e.g. category "Electronics" with fashion footwear description) are flagged for mandatory seller correction.

4
Community Reporting & Rapid Administrative Takedown Coming soon

An in-page community reporting trigger on every live listing is coming soon. Until it is available, please report misleading or inappropriate listings to the RichStore Trust & Safety Team directly; reported listings are quarantined into the Administrator Review Queue.

5. Seller Penalties & Strike System

Violations of this policy incur structured disciplinary actions based on severity:

Tier 1 — Warning & Listing Rejection (Minor Inadvertent Errors):

Blurry images or minor title discrepancies. The listing is rejected with an educational prompt detailing how to re-take clear photos and align the description.

Tier 2 — Temporary Account Suspension (Repeated Deceptive Listings):

Accumulation of 2 or more deceptive listings within 30 days. The seller account is suspended for 14 days and all active listings are unindexed.

Tier 3 — Permanent Blacklist & Legal Referral (Severe / Adult / Exploitation):

Posting pornography, adult novelties, sexually explicit imagery, or exploitation items results in immediate permanent account termination, verified phone forfeiture, and immediate referral to national security / cybercrime authorities.

6. Contact & Appeals

If a seller believes their product listing was incorrectly flagged or rejected by our automated system, they may request a human review by providing clear, high-resolution original photographs and authentic purchase provenance to the RichStore Trust & Safety Team.
